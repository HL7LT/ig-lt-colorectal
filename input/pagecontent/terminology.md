### Terminology

This page lists all CodeSystems and ValueSets defined in this Implementation Guide.

#### CodeSystems

- [Sydney Classification](CodeSystem-sydney-classification-cs.html) -- Sydney classification for deep mural injury (DMI) of the colon (Types I-V)
- [SNOMED CT Extension for Colorectal Screening](CodeSystem-snomed-extension-lt-colorectal.html) -- extension concepts for Paris classification, NICE classification, polyp access, tumor budding, PDC grading, MMRP expression, and invasion depth

#### ValueSets -- Colonoscopy Procedure

- [Colonoscope Reach](ValueSet-colonoscope-reach.html) -- anatomical structures identifying how far the colonoscope reached
- [Bowel Preparation Quality](ValueSet-bowel-preparation-quality.html) -- overall quality (effective, inadequate, not done)
- [Bowel Preparation Score Values](ValueSet-bowel-prep-score-values.html) -- Boston Bowel Preparation Scale segment scores (0-3)
- [BBPS Colon Segments](ValueSet-bbps-colon-segments.html) -- three segments evaluated in BBPS
- [Bowel Preparation Substances](ValueSet-bowel-preparation-substance.html) -- substances used for bowel preparation

#### ValueSets -- Polyp Findings

- [Paris Classification](ValueSet-paris-classification.html) -- Paris endoscopic classification of superficial neoplastic lesions
- [NICE Classification](ValueSet-nice-classification.html) -- NBI International Colorectal Endoscopic classification
- [Polyp Access](ValueSet-polyp-access.html) -- difficulty of access to the polyp (SMSA component)
- [Polyp Predicted Histology](ValueSet-polyp-predicted-histology.html) -- predicted histological type
- [Polypectomy Method](ValueSet-polypectomy-method.html) -- methods used for polypectomy

#### ValueSets -- Complications

- [Complication Presence](ValueSet-colonoscopy-complication-presence.html) -- yes/no
- [Complication Type](ValueSet-colonoscopy-complication-type.html) -- hemorrhage, anesthesia, perforation
- [Wall Injury Action](ValueSet-colonoscopy-wall-injury-action.html) -- clipping, sutured, primary operation
- [Sydney Classification](ValueSet-sydney-classification.html) -- Sydney DMI Types I-V

#### ValueSets -- Conclusions & FOBT

- [Colorectal Conclusion](ValueSet-colorectal-conclusion.html) -- colonoscopy conclusion findings (~30 coded findings)
- [FOBT Test Codes](ValueSet-fecal-occult-blood-test-codes.html) -- LOINC codes for FOBT
- [FOBT Qualitative Result](ValueSet-fecal-occult-blood-qualitative-result.html) -- detected / not detected

#### ValueSets -- Pathology / Histology

- [Histological Diagnosis](ValueSet-colorectal-histological-diagnosis.html) -- colorectal histological diagnosis categories
- [Dysplasia Grade](ValueSet-dysplasia-grade.html) -- dysplasia grading
- [Radicalism of Removal](ValueSet-radicalism-of-removal.html) -- R0/R1/RX classification
- [Tumor Budding](ValueSet-tumor-budding.html) -- tumor budding grades (Bd0-Bd3)
- [Invasion Depth](ValueSet-invasion-depth.html) -- Haggitt and Kikuchi classification
- [MMRP Expression](ValueSet-mmrp-expression.html) -- mismatch repair protein expression status
